## **API Endpoints Overview**

### [**​**](https://docs.mountsea.ai/introduction#gemini-ai-endpoints)**Gemini AI Endpoints**

- **Generate Image with Nana/Bananana Models**\
  `POST /gemini/image`\
  Create an image using **Nana** or **Bananana** models
- **Generate Video with Veo2/Veo3 Models**\
  `POST /gemini/video`\
  Create a video clip from a description or existing content
- **Get Video Task Status**\
  `GET /gemini/task`\
  Retrieve the current status of your video generation task
- **Advanced Editing Operations**\
  `POST /gemini/task`\
  Perform editing operations on generated content

### [**​**](https://docs.mountsea.ai/introduction#suno-ai-endpoints)**Suno AI Endpoints**

- **Generate Music**\
  `POST /suno/generate`\
  Create a new music track using Suno’s AI
- **Get Task Status**\
  `GET /suno/task`\
  Retrieve the status of your current task
- **Upload Music Files**\
  `POST /suno/upload`\
  Upload your audio files for processing
- **Generate Lyrics**\
  `POST /suno/lyrics`\
  Generate song lyrics from a prompt
- **Advanced Audio Operations**\
  `POST /suno/mp4` | `POST /suno/wav`\
  Perform operations such as converting audio formats or adjusting tempo
